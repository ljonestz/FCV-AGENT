==============================================================================
4. WHAT WAS BUILT (committed, offline, test-driven)
==============================================================================
scripts/climate_claim_adjudication_harness.py:
  - enumerate_claims(reader) -> deterministic sentence-level claim inventory
    (claim_id = "<object_id>#p<para>#s<sent>"); no judgement, so no empty-set
    escape hatch.
  - adjudicate_claims(inventory, source_registry, adjudication) -> validates a
    per-claim judgement and emits the existing repair-pipeline "review". Enforces:
    every claim judged exactly once (coverage); a contradicted/not_established
    FACTUAL assertion MUST be a defect (closes recall hatch); a supported claim
    cannot be a defect; a supported factual_assertion MUST cite >=1 exact
    entailing source quote (closes the "mark all supported" hatch); defect spans
    must be unique substrings; source_ids must exist; qualified inference /
    proposed actions may be non-defects and may omit support quotes (preserves
    valid analysis).
  - score_against_expected(result, expected) -> {passed, missed_defects,
    false_positives}. passed = all true defects found AND zero false positives.
Tests: tests/test_climate_claim_adjudication_harness.py (13, all green with the
prototype + replay suites: 27 passed). Design rationale in the decision doc.
