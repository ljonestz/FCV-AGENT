# Targeted source review: two candidate corrections for human review

The user approved proceeding with targeted source checks and human review after the
complete replay missed both original major errors. This is an offline application
of that approach, not another detection experiment or production integration.

## Review method

For each consequential claim, compare the assertion with the cited source's exact
subject, population/site, activity, delivery stage and claimed effect. Record what
the passage establishes and what it leaves unestablished. Naming an instrument or
matching terminology is insufficient. Use the weakest accurate wording that retains
the useful analysis; do not replace uncertainty with a claim of absence or failure.

Two specific checks address the observed misses:

| Check | Evidence needed to sustain the stronger assertion | Handling when missing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delivered protection or immediate realized benefit | Evidence of completion/operation or measured benefit for the relevant infrastructure and period | Describe intended benefit/design or hold the assertion for review |
| Specific protection against exclusion | A described measure linked to the relevant exclusion risk and population; implementation/effectiveness evidence only if those are asserted | Credit documented risk recognition and general instruments without asserting specific protection |

A consequential false premise also holds the advice that depends on it. Reasonable
recommendations and qualified risks do not need to be existing commitments. Hedging
does not rescue a contradicted premise. Minor label, rounding or phrasing differences
remain nonblocking when they do not change a substantive decision. Missing source
IDs and placeholder quotes are evidence failures; obtain real support or remove the
unsupported citation, never invent replacement evidence.
