## Requested behavior

- Normal FCV and Climate share the left rating sidebar in Summary and Detailed.
- Climate sensitivity and responsiveness use five levels; responsiveness measures
  relevant wider peace/social dividends and often goes above and beyond. Unclear
  and Not expected are unscored; saved Emerging stays legacy. Normal FCV's values
  are unchanged. Sidebar explanations are short; rationale is collapsed.
- Climate Summary shows up to five substantial, qualified gap bullets between
  strengths and priorities. Gap coverage is independent of surviving actions.
- All admitted actions, up to five, remain accessible and exportable. There is no
  recommendation quota. A withheld action is never restored to fill a number.
- Complete supporting gap prose is in Detailed, standalone HTML and Word.
- Machine markers, excessive legacy paragraph spacing, regional scope/research
  handoff, unsupported role claims and unproven source dates are guarded.

## Evidence

Final frozen suite: **1,443 passed in 354.52s**. Affected connected checks: 104 passed.
Synthetic one/three/five-action cases cover separate drafting, review, ranking,
JSON and HTML/Word. Real browser checks cover second actions, both views, saving,
standalone HTML, phone/desktop overflow and marker cleanup. The final real-result
presentation replay has five full gaps and two unchanged reviewed actions.

Two additional paid runs were used, within the user's allowance. The first failed
source/analysis handoff and was not accepted. Repairs were made and tested before
the second, which retained ten responses, six pathways, seven gaps and two of four
proposed actions. Two national-context quotes match public World Bank sources for
Burundi and DRC. Four other claims and two unsupported actions were withheld.

Final prompt clarifications were added after that trial and checked offline; they
have not had another paid generation. Deterministic readout/source safeguards and
export parity were checked against the preserved result without regrading it.
