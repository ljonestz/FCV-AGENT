## Decision boundary and method

This is a substantive source review, not human approval. Every human approval remains pending. No candidate, source register, review status, reviewer, approval date, or due date was changed. The stored country-level review due dates are expired and were not renewed.

I checked each evidence statement against the cited primary-source page or document passage, then checked each pathway against its supporting evidence IDs. The review tests documentary scope, causal strength, geography, time period, uncertainty, and locator accuracy. Historical project and crisis findings remain historical; verification of an old passage does not make it a current 2026 condition. Publisher HTML, official PDFs, indexed publisher text, and official alternate-language pages were used where direct pages returned access errors. Indexed text counted as sufficient only when it exposed the official URL, named section, continuous relevant passage, and the claim's figures or qualifications. A short search snippet alone did not receive a retain verdict. No source PDF is included or cleared for redistribution.

Verdicts mean:

- `RETAIN`: the stored statement is substantively supported with its existing uncertainty.
- `REVISE`: the record can remain only after the exact correction shown below.
- `DEFER`: the cited evidence does not presently support an approvable climate-FCV record.

## South Sudan profile synthesis

Verdict: `REVISE`.

The coverage matrix, geographic notes, sector note 1, and all four known gaps are supportable. Two profile sentences overstate the underlying evidence:

1. Executive assessment 1 calls the severe-year flood estimate "observed". Exact replacement: "The bank establishes a robust direction of future warming and explicit precipitation uncertainty from South Sudan CMIP6 views, alongside a CCDR severe-year flood-exposure estimate; it still lacks a complete extracted set of numeric hazard magnitudes across scenarios, horizons, extremes, and subnational areas."
2. Executive assessment 3 says selected programs "demonstrate" capacities. Exact replacement: "Official reporting from selected safety-net and community-governance programs documents household outcomes, digital-delivery tools, local-planning bodies, and infrastructure outputs, while third-party dependence, limited geographic reach, and the lack of independent evaluation prevent national generalization."
3. Sector note 2 should use the same calibration. Exact replacement: "Official monitoring reports selected safety-net, local-governance, infrastructure, and flood-protection results, including delivery-system and third-party implementation lessons; no independent evaluation establishes causal effectiveness, durability, or unintended effects."
