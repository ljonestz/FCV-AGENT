# Climate decision acceptance benchmark

## Purpose and method

Use the Great Lakes AF appraisal PID in test_documents as the demanding regional
case. This benchmark describes decisions to assess, not recommendations to insert.
Inspect project facts, credited responses, residual gaps and final independently
accepted actions. Similar words or a recommendation count alone do not establish
quality. Smaller conceptual documents may legitimately support fewer actions.

The saved quality6 baseline has four candidates and two accepted actions. Keep
that result unchanged. Offline transport fixtures show one, three and five actions
can survive; they do not establish that fresh model generation meets this benchmark.

## Acceptance of a fresh run

- At least three distinct, material, independently accepted operational priorities
  for this demanding case. Each must have a project anchor, residual decision,
  feasible action, proportionate scope and a credible climate/FCV mechanism.
  Three administrative checks do not pass. Do not restore a rejected action or
  manufacture a gap to meet the number; a shortfall keeps rollout held.
- Existing controls receive credit, including funded climate-smart support.
  Opportunities for peace/social benefits are conditional and within scope;
  missing dividends terminology is never a criticism or a required objective.
- Optional wording may be withheld while a separately reviewed decision survives.
  Corrections use the matching native schema and independent review, preserve
  evidence/rank/scope and remain inside deadline and shared token allowance.
- Research uses received passage IDs, faithful quotes/URLs and source-owned project
  labels. Cover both beneficiary countries. Retain incomplete proof for inspection,
  withhold it from admitted prompt grounding, and show the evidence limitation.
- Native Summary and three prose findings are compiled after action review, cite
  admitted support, and pass a separate meaning check. No planned/completed reversal,
  altered measured quantity, invented actor or unaccepted recommendation.
- Summary gaps provide four or five substantive, qualified points when supported.
  Paired short sidebar ratings remain five-point scales. All priority cards open;
  recommendation details and sections04-06 closed. HTML and Word agree and contain
  no machine markers or obsolete asymmetric rating readout.

## Required evidence before rollout

Offline schema/cancellation/budget/provenance tests and full regression suite;
branch-test deployed SHA and health; saved-session presentation/export checks;
then one separately authorized fresh paid assessment with assessment ID, bounded
per-call model/usage diagnostics and a source-based content review. A saved replay
cannot substitute for the final generation check. Production/main/ITS remain held.
