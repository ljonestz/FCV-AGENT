# Source review: Haiti and Papua New Guinea

Reviewed 26 October 2026 against country-bank commit `12a804fe92bacfdaf0bec7926725d8a7a9376fe4`. Scope is the canonical `2026.08` candidates for Haiti (HTI) and Papua New Guinea (PNG): 22 source-register entries, 30 evidence records, 12 pathways, and both profiles. This is a substantive review packet, not human approval. No review date was renewed, no candidate status was changed, and no record was promoted.

## Method and decision rule

I opened every registered URL and inspected the cited passage or the closest publisher-hosted passage available, then compared the stored statement, compact statement, uncertainty, causal strength, geography, time period, and locator. `RETAIN` means no mandatory evidence-record field edit is needed; it does not make a dated observation current in 2026. `REVISE` means the next candidate must edit a statement, compact statement, uncertainty, status, `source_refs`, or locator before approval. Source-register-only corrections are reported separately and do not by themselves change an evidence decision. `DEFER` would mean the claim could not be checked sufficiently. Historical and programme-document claims remain historical or intended even where retained.

Two Haiti PDFs could not be opened in full. The climate-profile URL returned HTTP 403; its relevant printed-page-4 “Climate baseline summary” and “Climate future” text, not the full PDF, was reproduced in the search index for the same World Bank-hosted file with the exact-text queries `"Climate baseline summary for Haiti (since 1960)"`, `"Rainfall projections project decreases in rainfall during June-August" Haiti`, and `"Temperatures are expected to increase by 0.5 to 2.3" Haiti climate profile`. The result linked to the [registered World Bank PDF](https://climateknowledgeportal.worldbank.org/sites/default/files/2018-10/wb_gfdrr_climate_change_country_profile_for_HTI.pdf). Search extraction can vary; the current [Haiti CCKP resources page](https://climateknowledgeportal.worldbank.org/country/haiti/resources) confirms the publisher context but does not reproduce the old profile. The National Adaptation Plan exceeded the browser's document-size limit; its complete relevant printed-page-16 passage was exposed under the registered UNFCCC URL by `site:unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/PNA_HAITI.pdf "21 actions" adaptation Haiti agriculture health infrastructure water`. Thus HTI-E-001, HTI-E-003 and HTI-E-012 were checked against their complete cited passages, not their full documents. No TLS or security control was bypassed. Public availability was not treated as proof of an open licence.

## Source access and metadata findings

- Coverage: 20 registered URLs were opened directly. HTI-SRC-004 returned HTTP 403 and HTI-SRC-002 exceeded the browser size limit; only their complete relevant indexed passages (printed pp. 4 and 16 respectively) were checked, using the reproducible queries and publisher links above. All 30 evidence records and 12 pathways received a decision. No record is deferred because HTI-E-001/003/012 depend only on those inspected passages, but the limitations must remain visible.
- HTI-SRC-009 displays publication date **2 April 2026**. Replace `publication_date: null` with `2026-04-02` and `publication_date_basis: not-stated` with `publication`. The separate evidence-locator edit is specified under HTI-E-008.
- HTI-SRC-005 now records CSP Revision 01 approved in June 2026. Preserve the original November 2023 approval/document date but update version/temporal notes so a reviewer can distinguish the revised live page from the original plan.
- PNG-SRC-004 is publicly downloadable, but its copyright page says all rights are reserved and prior written permission is required for reproduction. Replace `license_status: open` with the valid schema value `permission-required`, recording the copyright-page basis. This is a metadata classification from the source notice, not broader legal advice.
- PNG-SRC-003's displayed technical-assistance notice restricts disclosure beyond enumerated recipients absent consent. Public download alone does not establish an open reuse licence; reclassify `license_status` to `unknown` unless a separate IMF licence is recorded.
- Other `license_status: open` values were not treated as certified merely because files were publicly reachable. Before approval, retain “open” only where the source itself or publisher terms provide an explicit reuse licence; otherwise use `unknown`. PNG-SRC-001 explicitly states CC BY-ND 4.0, PNG-SRC-010 CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, and PNG-SRC-011 CC BY 3.0 IGO.
- PNG-E-011's printed p. 28 is PDF p. 41; PNG-E-012's printed p. 30 is PDF p. 43. Record both numbering systems. PNG-E-006's current IOM locator is materially wrong and must be replaced as specified above.
- Both candidate `review_due` dates are expired. This review does not renew them. Existing record-level `review_status: reviewed` fields and profile review flags must not be read as current human approval.
