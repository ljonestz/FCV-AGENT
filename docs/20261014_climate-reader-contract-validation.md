## Implemented contract

- Text-only reader_correction returns exact requested IDs and paragraphs. Application
  merge preserves IDs, themes, evidence and every untargeted record. Invalid scope,
  missing/duplicate/unknown targets, metadata and new numbers/dates fail specifically.
- Native prechecks retain schema/plain text, reference, number/date and word limits.
  Sentence capitalization and vocabulary/verb guesses no longer decide native
  meaning. The strict normal FCV Summary route retains its previous behavior.
- Independent clean meaning review remains mandatory, including after correction.
  It receives original corrected records and their restricted cited support. Actors,
  actions, units, negation, status and source scope belong to that review. Incomplete
  or malformed pass, block, provider failure or exhausted repair allowance withholds
  native prose while retaining already accepted actions.
- Initial response, exact prechecks, patch/targets, merge reasons and review outcomes
  are bounded private diagnostics. Reader, saved-result admission and exports exclude
  them. Irreparable metadata/reference failures skip a pointless text repair.
- One correction and fresh review share the unchanged deadline and 9,000-token
  allowance with zero repair retries. No dependency, UI or production change.
