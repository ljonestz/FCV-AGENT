## Method and decision rule

I checked all 32 evidence records, all 12 pathways, both profile syntheses, and all 25 cited sources against the cited primary publication. A record is `RETAIN` when its wording, scope, status, uncertainty and locator are adequate; `REVISE` when a specific field must change before approval; and `DEFER` when the cited material could not support a responsible decision. Historical evidence is assessed for what it establishes at its own date, not treated as current verification in October 2026. Page references below distinguish PDF pages from printed report pages where they differ.

The current source set supports decisions on most records. One Yemen evidence record and one Yemen pathway are deferred. This does not clear either country for approval: both country-level `review_due` dates have expired, and this review does not renew them.
