## Repair and verification method

- Received citations now take precedence over metadata-only snippets. Later
  beneficiary-country proof can survive the four-source cap; one whole passage
  per retained source is reserved within the existing twelve-passage cap. Country
  labels must occur in received proof/title. No geography, quote or source is invented.
- Fact extraction requires same population, status, period, unit, denominator and
  scope before calling statements contradictory. Conditional review must verify
  the residual-gap premise; an unsupported premise rejects its parent action.
- Structurally valid readers with admitted references reach initial meaning review
  despite repairable text defects. The single correction receives both mechanical
  and semantic issues. Block or malformed reviews do not consume a correction.
- Length repairs receive per-paragraph ceilings totaling 220 words. The admission
  ceiling remains 260. Meaning-only repairs are not assigned this shorter budget.
  Initial prose prefers two connected paragraphs around 190 words and a bridge to
  priority categories rather than a catalogue of every action.
- The application still owns evidence, identifiers and untouched records. After
  correction, mechanical acceptance and a fresh complete clean meaning review are
  required. Shared deadline/token allowance and zero correction retries remain.
