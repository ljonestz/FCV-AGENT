## Method and implemented boundaries

- Operational decisions receive core-only source, scope, authority, numeric and
  admission checks. Optional wording is validated separately. Native semantic
  review must explicitly accept each core ID; unknown/missing acceptance fails closed.
- Draft artifacts have independent IDs. A rejected paragraph cannot erase its
  accepted parent. Core/draft repairs use their matching schema, preserve evidence,
  score and rank, and require independent re-review. Repairs share 9,000 output tokens
  with no transient repair retry. Unknown usage consumes the requested allowance.
- Optional drafting reserves 120s for mandatory core review. Provider failures in
  optional corrections/prose retain independently accepted actions; cancellation
  propagates. Reader calls remain within the original 14-minute wall-clock cap.
- Search citations become bounded application-owned passages. Classifier selects
  IDs and exact uploaded project labels. Quotes, URLs and dates are not regenerated.
  Source-label matches require complete spans and keep DRC/Republic of Congo distinct.
  Pure classification sends a bounded packet; encrypted blocks remain only in search
  continuation. Failed quorum/coverage proof is retained for inspection and excluded
  from both admitted prompt paths. Unavailable bank reasons remain explicit.
- Native judgment defers prose. Final Summary/three findings compile after action
  review, bind to admitted evidence/accepted actions and receive independent meaning
  review. Reader 2.16 exports reviewed narratives, rechecks support and handles a
  missing reader with a clear limited fallback. Separate top-level prose cannot
  substitute for semantically reviewed narratives. Legacy saves are unchanged.
- Metrics record configured/resolved model, each returned/failed attempt, scope and
  available usage. Unknown usage is not estimated. Live cost savings are unmeasured.

## Offline evidence

Initial reproductions failed as expected for native correction without drafting,
invalid optional wording, artifact rejection, provider failure, lost partial proof,
missing core pass IDs and mutable export narratives. Corresponding assertions now
pass. Synthetic one/three/five-action tests exercise transport and rendering only.

Two bounded read-only Sol reviews found and resolved the contract, prompt-grounding,
provider-failure, export-provenance, deadline and telemetry issues. GPT-5.6 Luna was
unavailable. Reviews did not execute tests or call the app's paid provider.

The first full run passed 1,490 tests and exposed three outdated fixtures. They
expected pre-admission narratives, six rather than eight stages, and single-source
prompt admission. All 72 checks in those fixture modules passed after alignment.
Final full regression: **1,493 passed in 912.27 seconds**, including the aligned
fixtures and the final source-label/export boundary cases.
