# Conflict-Sensitive Climate Action in FCV Settings Compendium

## Authority and role
World Bank compendium of pilots embedded in natural-resource and climate operations in Chad, Burkina Faso, and Mali. It supplies operational evidence on design, participation, access constraints, and adaptive management.

## Key propositions
- Map primary, seasonal, mobile, displaced, host, and secondary users before making siting, boundary, access, or benefit-sharing decisions.
- Management committees, communications, and participation rules shape legitimacy and sustainability; consultation limited to safer areas can bias whose interests are heard.
- Early screening reduces but does not eliminate later disputes. Grievance and monitoring information should function as management and early-warning inputs.
- Flexible sequencing, locally grounded delivery, and explicit adjustment triggers help operations respond to changing access and conflict conditions.

## Questions supported
Power and distribution, delivery feasibility, resource access, disaster and services, participation and grievance mechanisms, and delivery-side dividends.

## Recommendation implications
Stress-test site selection, resource rules, committee composition, partner coverage, and grievance data use. Convert conflict-sensitivity commitments into routines, owners, information flows, and adjustment decisions.

## Caveats
The three pilots are context-specific and should inform questions, not be copied as solutions. CERC references remain instrument- and policy-dependent.
