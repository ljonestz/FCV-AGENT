# Defueling Conflict

## Authority and role
World Bank (2022) analytical and operational source on environment and natural-resource management as a pathway to peace. It anchors mediated resource pathways and the upstream/downstream questionnaire.

## Key propositions
- Natural resources influence conflict through institutions, access, incentives, political economy, livelihoods, mobility, and conflict economies; scarcity alone is not a sufficient mechanism.
- The same resource intervention can create grievances or cooperation depending on rights, benefit distribution, legitimacy, participation, and dispute resolution.
- Analysis should connect conflict impacts on resources with resource impacts on conflict and distinguish peace opportunities from unsupported peace claims.
- Upstream analytics and downstream operations require linked questions on actors, resource users, geography, gender, displacement, land-use change, and implementation risk.

## Questions supported
Project climate influence, power and distribution, resource access, livelihoods, mobility, mitigation and transition, and transboundary systems.

## Recommendation implications
Name the resource mechanism, users, rights, incentives, geography, and decision point. Test conflict-economy exposure and build transparent allocation, benefit-sharing, dispute resolution, and monitoring where material.

## Caveats
Environmental peacebuilding is not automatic. General NRM relevance activates the Climate lens only when a clear climate-related mechanism is present.
