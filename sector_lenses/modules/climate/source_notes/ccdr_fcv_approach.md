# Promoting FCV-Sensitive Climate Action in CCDRs

## Authority and role
World Bank approach note for integrating FCV into Country Climate and Development Report analysis and recommendations. It anchors Climate materiality, contextual evidence, modelling caveats, and recommendation screening.

## Key propositions
- Use three linked steps: profile climate-FCV interactions and differentiated exposure; incorporate uncertainty and FCV pathways into modelling; and screen recommendations for conflict sensitivity and contextual fit.
- National modelling and aggregate findings require subnational, group, institutional, and political-economy interpretation.
- Scenario analysis helps test interventions under uncertainty rather than treating forecasts as precise.
- Recommendations should identify winners and losers, screen maladaptation and Do No Harm risks, and vary with FCV conditions.

## Questions supported
Materiality, project climate influence, compound risk, differentiated effects, maladaptation, and conditional mitigation or transition analysis.

## Recommendation implications
Use CCDR insights as contextual evidence only when they sharpen a concrete mechanism or action. Do not infer project applicability from national findings and do not recommend consulting a CCDR as a routine action.

## Caveats
CCDR evidence does not replace submitted-project evidence. Model assumptions, data gaps, future conflict pathways, and geographic aggregation must remain visible.
